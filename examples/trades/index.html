trades dashboard — live

10 000 limit orders over a rolling window, each { id, bid, ask }. A drifting regime drives the tick stream — mid-price wanders ±$2 around $75 on one sine wave, half-spread bias cycles tight↔wide on another, bid/ask imbalance oscillates on a third, all incommensurate. Each tick replaces one row's bid or ask with a value drawn from the current regime. Four reactive views per card — liquidCount, avg bid, plus an order book driven by two length(fn) views grouping trades by bid- and ask-price across 15 price levels. Depth chart (cumulative staircase) on the left, price / qty / cum ladder on the right. The cpu wave plots wall-clock per frame over the last 200 frames (60-frame rolling average) against a shared y-axis. Same tick stream fed to every lib; toggle libs on/off with the chips below.
