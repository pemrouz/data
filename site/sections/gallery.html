Write the view, flow the change

An eight-section essay on the duality at the core of the engine: a table and its stream of changes are the same thing in two forms, so a view — a filter, a count, a grouping — stays live for the price of the change, not the table — O(Δ), not O(N) — flowing only through the views that read it, down to a single minimal DOM instruction. The readability of recompute, the cost of hand-tuned deltas, and none of the bookkeeping.
